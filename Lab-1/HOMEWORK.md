## The task

Pick one small feature. A thin slice of your capstone, or something CampusPulse-adjacent. Small means buildable in an evening or two.

1. **Spec it** (`spec.md`, one page). Use the Lab 1 template: problem, users, success criteria, context list, acceptance criteria, napkin math. Write the spec before the code. That order is the assignment.
2. **Build it with an agentic workflow.** Your repo folder needs an `AGENTS.md` (copy your team's). Direct your tool from the spec. You may write code by hand too; the point is deliberate delegation, not maximum delegation.
3. **Verify it** (`golden.md`). Three golden questions: input, expected behavior, actual result, pass or fail. If one fails, fixing it or documenting why not is both acceptable. Hiding it is not.
4. **Log the delegation** (`delegation.md`, half a page). What you handed to AI, what came back wrong, how you caught it. Honest logs score higher than heroic ones.
5. **Attach your Pattern Journal** covering Weeks 1 to 4 (one short entry per week).
