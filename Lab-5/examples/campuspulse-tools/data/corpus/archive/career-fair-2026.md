# Archive: Spring Career Fair 2026

## Summary
The spring career fair took place in the Auditorium foyer with 18 companies from Georgia and abroad. Around 400 students attended over the day.

## Lessons for organisers
Queues formed at the entrance because sign-in took too long. Next year the Events Office will open online registration two weeks ahead and print badges in advance.

## Next fair
The next career fair is planned for spring 2027. The date has not been announced yet.
