# Archive: Autumn Hackathon 2025

## Summary
The 2025 autumn hackathon ran over a weekend in Building C with 14 teams. The winning team built a timetable clash checker for students taking modules from two faculties.

## What went wrong
The Wi-Fi in Building C struggled with over sixty laptops at once. For 2026 the Startup Society has asked IT for a temporary extra access point.

## Prizes
Prizes were sponsored by local companies. The 2026 prize list will be published when registration opens.
