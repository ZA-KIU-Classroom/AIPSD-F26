# Archive: Open Build Night, September 2026

## What happened
The Robotics Club's September Open Build Night drew 27 students to Lab B204, close to the room's limit. Most were first-year students. Teams built line-following robots from starter kits in about ninety minutes.

## What changed afterwards
Because the room was almost full, the club now asks people to register for Open Build Night on the noticeboard sign-up sheet. Walk-ins are still accepted while there is space.

## Feedback
Attendees asked for more soldering stations and for a beginner track. The October session adds a beginner table with pre-soldered kits.
