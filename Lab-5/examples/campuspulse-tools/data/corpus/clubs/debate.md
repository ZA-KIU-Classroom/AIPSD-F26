# KIU Debate Society

## About
The Debate Society trains students in British Parliamentary debate and sends teams to regional tournaments in Tbilisi and Batumi. Debates are held in English. Students of all years can join.

## Meetings
Practice debates run every Tuesday at 19:00 in Room A112. Motions are announced 15 minutes before the round starts, so you prepare on the spot.

## Tournaments
The society selects two teams each semester for external tournaments. Selection is based on attendance at practice and a short internal round in the fourth week of the semester. Travel costs for selected teams are covered by the Student Affairs club budget.

## Membership
There is no membership fee. New members are asked to attend two practice debates as observers before they speak in a round.
