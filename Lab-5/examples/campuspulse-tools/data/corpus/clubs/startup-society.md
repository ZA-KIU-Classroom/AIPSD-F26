# KIU Startup Society

## About
The Startup Society helps students turn ideas into products. It runs pitch nights, founder talks and a weekend hackathon each semester. Members come from computer science, management and mathematics.

## Meetings
Founder talks are held on the first Wednesday of each month at 19:00 in the Auditorium. Pitch nights are announced on the noticeboard at least two weeks ahead.

## Hackathon
The autumn hackathon runs from Friday 13 November 2026 at 18:00 to Sunday 15 November 2026 at 16:00 in Building C. Teams have two to four members. Registration closes on Friday 6 November 2026.

## Membership
Membership is free for KIU students. Alumni may attend founder talks as guests.
