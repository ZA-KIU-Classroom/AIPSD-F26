# KIU Volunteering Circle

## About
The Volunteering Circle organises tutoring for school students in Kutaisi, park clean-ups and a winter clothing drive. Volunteers can join single events or commit for the whole semester.

## Meetings
A planning meeting takes place every other Thursday at 18:30 in Room A015. The schedule of upcoming volunteering events is shared at each meeting.

## Requirements
Volunteers who tutor school students must attend a two-hour orientation on working with minors before their first session. Orientation runs twice per semester.

## Membership
There is no fee. Volunteers receive a certificate of hours at the end of the semester.
