# KIU Chess Club

## About
The Chess Club welcomes players of every level, from beginners who are still learning how the knight moves to rated tournament players.

## Meetings
Casual play every Monday and Thursday from 17:00 to 20:00 in the student lounge on the ground floor of Building A. Boards and clocks are provided.

## Tournaments
A rapid tournament is held on the last Friday of each month. Entry is free, and the time control is 15 minutes plus 10 seconds per move.

## Membership
There is no fee and no sign-up. Turn up and play.
