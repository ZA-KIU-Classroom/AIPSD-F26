# KIU Photography Club

## About
The Photography Club runs photo walks around Kutaisi, editing workshops and an end-of-semester exhibition in the main hall. Any camera works, including a phone.

## Meetings
The club meets every second Saturday at 11:00, starting from the main entrance. Most meetings are photo walks of two to three hours, so wear comfortable shoes.

## Equipment
The club owns two mirrorless cameras and a set of lenses that members can borrow for one week at a time. Borrowing requires a deposit of 50 GEL, which is returned when the equipment comes back undamaged.

## Membership
Membership costs 20 GEL per semester. The fee pays for printing the exhibition photos.
