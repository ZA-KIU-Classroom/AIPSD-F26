# KIU Robotics Club

## About
The KIU Robotics Club builds small autonomous machines: line followers, robotic arms and, this year, a delivery rover for the campus courtyard. The club is open to students from every year and every faculty. First-year students are welcome, and no prior experience with electronics is needed.

## Meetings
The club meets every Wednesday at 18:00 in Lab B204. Meetings run for about two hours. The first half is a short talk or tutorial, the second half is build time in small teams.

## Open Build Night
Once a month the club runs an Open Build Night for anyone curious. The next one is on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 18:00 in Lab B204. Laptops, soldering stations and starter kits are provided, so you do not need to bring your own equipment.

## Membership
Membership is free. To join, come to any Wednesday meeting and add your name to the member list. Members who want to use the soldering stations outside meeting hours must first complete the club's one-hour safety induction.

## Contact
Club lead: robotics@clubs.kiu.example. The club posts updates on the student noticeboard outside Lab B204.
