# Academic Calendar Extract, Fall 2026

## Teaching weeks
The fall semester has fifteen calendar weeks. Weeks 8 and 9 are the midterm examination period: there are no regular classes, and clubs should avoid scheduling large events in those weeks.

## Club activity during exams
Club meetings may continue during the midterm period, but rooms are booked for exams first. Club bookings in weeks 8 and 9 can be cancelled at 48 hours notice if a room is needed for an exam.

## Public holidays
The university is closed on 23 November 2026 (Saint George's Day). No rooms can be booked on public holidays.
