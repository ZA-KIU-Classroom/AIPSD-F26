# Student Club Policy

## Registration
A student club must have at least eight members and one faculty advisor to register. Registration is renewed at the start of every academic year.

## Fees
Clubs may charge a membership fee of up to 30 GEL per semester. Any fee must be listed on the club's page, together with what it pays for. Clubs may not charge students to attend an event that is advertised as open to all.

## Funding
Each registered club can apply to Student Affairs for up to 1,500 GEL per semester. Applications close in the third week of each semester. Unspent money does not carry over to the next semester.

## Events with outside guests
Events with more than ten guests from outside KIU need approval from Student Affairs at least ten working days in advance. Guests must sign in at the main entrance.

## Food and drink
Food is allowed in rooms A112 and A015 and in the Auditorium foyer. Food and drink are not allowed in any lab, including Lab B204.
