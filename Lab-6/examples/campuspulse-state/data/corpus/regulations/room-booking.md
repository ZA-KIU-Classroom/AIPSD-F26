# Room Booking Rules for Student Clubs

## Who can book
Registered student clubs can book teaching rooms and labs through the Student Affairs office. Individual students cannot book rooms for club activities in their own name.

## How far in advance
Rooms for events that start after 17:00 must be booked at least five working days in advance. Rooms for daytime events must be booked at least two working days in advance. Bookings made later than this are accepted only if the room is free and Student Affairs agrees.

## Room capacities
Room A112 seats 40 people. Lab B204 seats 30 people and holds the club soldering stations. Room A015 seats 20 people. The Auditorium seats 220 people and must be booked through the Events Office, not Student Affairs.

## Closing time
All teaching rooms and labs close at 22:00 on weekdays. Events must end and the room must be cleared by 21:45.

## Weekends
Rooms are not bookable on Sundays except with written approval from the Head of Student Affairs. Saturday bookings follow the normal rules, but buildings close at 18:00 on Saturdays.
